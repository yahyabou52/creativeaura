# AURA process artwork

Tool: built-in image generation. Identity references: `public/assets/aura-face.png` and `public/assets/aura-mascot.png`.

Shared prompt: Create one isolated premium 3D AURA website illustration on a truly transparent background, square composition. Preserve the reference character's soft tactile curled cobalt, sky blue and white flower/flame petals, cream round face, huge glossy blue eyes, pink blush and tiny blue hands. Match reference lighting, materials and proportions. Fill 90% of the square with a complete silhouette and readable prop. No background, dark disk, text, labels or watermark.

- `public/assets/aura-discovery.png`: AURA holding two interlocking translucent aqua rings beneath the chin, inquisitive raised eyebrow, attentive eyes looking toward the rings, small thoughtful closed smile. Dominant face, clearly visible rings.
- `public/assets/aura-design.png`: AURA embracing a luminous chunky five-point cyan star beneath the chin, one glossy eye open and one playful wink, delighted asymmetric grin. Dominant face, clearly visible star.
- `public/assets/aura-launch.png`: AURA riding or lifting a chunky translucent pale-blue arrow pointing diagonally up-right beneath the chin, both eyes joyfully narrowed, excited open smile, petals swept slightly with the movement. Dominant face, clearly visible arrow.
